# Resummetry template and mobile QA checklist

## Templates
Test each template with:
- long name and long job title
- three experience entries with 4-6 bullet lines each
- 2 education entries
- 10+ skills
- long URLs
- missing optional sections
- two-page content
- profile photo where supported

Verify section headings stay with content, dates do not collide, columns remain balanced, links wrap, and no text is clipped.

Templates covered by the renderer: Professional, Modern, Minimal, Executive, Corporate, Strategic, CleanTech, Contemporary, Elegant, Refined, Classic.

## Mobile
At widths around 390px and 768px verify:
- the section editor is usable without horizontal page-level overflow
- editor/preview toggle is always reachable
- resume canvas can be panned/scroll-zoomed without breaking the toolbar
- next/previous controls remain visible
- exit and autosave status remain accessible
- template selection is scrollable
- text inputs and add/remove actions do not overflow
